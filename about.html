About Tat:vm

Mumbai, built with patience.

Tat:vm Builders & Developers is a Mumbai real estate development company — residential, commercial and mixed-use — built on five fundamentals.

Who we are

Rooted in Mumbai. Building for its next fifty years.

Every Tat:vm project begins with the same question: how will this space be lived in, ten years from today? The answer shapes our layouts, our light, our materials and our promises.

We are a young company with old-school discipline. Founded in 2025 and headquartered in Lower Parel, we work across Ghatkopar and central Mumbai — on new development and on redevelopment of aging housing societies, where the city needs it most.
