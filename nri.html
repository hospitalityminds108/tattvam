The investment case

Why invest in India. Why invest in Mumbai.

Two questions every NRI buyer asks us first, answered plainly, before we talk about any one home.

Why invest in India.

  • A large, still-young market

    One of the world's biggest populations means decades of housing demand still ahead of it.

  • Property as real, tangible value

    Real estate remains one of the most trusted ways Indian and NRI families hold wealth across generations.

  • Simple, NRI-friendly rules

    Under FEMA, NRIs and OCIs can buy residential property freely, with no RBI approval required.

Why invest in Mumbai.

  • India's financial capital

    Head offices, markets and jobs keep pulling people, and rental demand, toward the city.

  • Infrastructure on the move

    New metro lines, the coastal road and airport expansion are steadily opening up commute times.

  • Land that doesn't get made twice

    Mumbai's geography caps new supply, scarcity that has historically protected long-term value.

Frequently asked

NRI questions, answered.

Can NRIs buy residential property in Mumbai?

Yes. Under FEMA, NRIs and OCIs can purchase residential property in India without RBI approval. We help you stay compliant at every step.

Can I buy without visiting India?

Mostly, yes, with a Power of Attorney, video walkthroughs and digital documentation. We support the POA drafting and attestation process.

How do payments work from abroad?

Funds move through NRE/NRO accounts or inward remittance via banking channels. We share a clear payment schedule before you commit.

Can Tat:vm manage the home afterwards?

Yes, handover, furnishing coordination and rental management support is available for NRI buyers.
