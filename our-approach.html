Where we specialise

Two kinds of Mumbai, one discipline.

Ground-up projects are only half the city's story. We build for the half that's already lived-in, too.

01

SRA redevelopment

Under Maharashtra's SRA scheme, families living in recognised slum settlements can move into free, formally-titled homes, built by a developer who earns the right to sell additional flats on the same land. It's a policy built on patience: eligibility surveys, consent-building and phased construction, done right. We approach SRA the way we approach every project, with transparent eligibility documentation, realistic timelines, and homes built to the same five fundamentals as anything else we deliver.

02

Housing society redevelopment

Most of Mumbai's best land is already built on, by ageing societies whose buildings have outlived their structural life. Redevelopment lets those families stay rooted in the neighbourhood they know, in a new building with modern amenities, at no cost to them, while the developer monetises the additional FSI. We run it as a five-step, fully consent-driven process, society engagement, feasibility, approvals, construction and handover, each stage documented and shared, so no resident is guessing where their building stands.
