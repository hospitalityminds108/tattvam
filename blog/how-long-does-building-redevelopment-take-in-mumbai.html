Building redevelopment in Mumbai has become a necessity due to aging structures, safety concerns, and the growing demand for modern living spaces. One of the most common questions society members and homeowners ask is:

“How long does building redevelopment take in Mumbai?”

At Tatvm Group, we believe transparency is key. This guide breaks down the realistic timeline of redevelopment projects in Mumbai, the stages involved, and the factors that influence project duration.

Average Time for Building Redevelopment in Mumbai

On average, a building redevelopment in Mumbai takes 2 to 5 years from the initial planning stage to possession. However, the exact duration depends on multiple factors such as approvals, project size, legal clearances, and construction complexity.

Step-by-Step Timeline of Building Redevelopment

1. Society Consent & Feasibility Study

Time Required: 3 to 6 months

The redevelopment process begins when:

Society members pass a redevelopment resolution

A feasibility study is conducted

A developer like Tatvm Group is shortlisted

This stage includes legal checks, land verification, and understanding redevelopment potential under DCR rules.

2. Appointment of Developer & Legal Documentation

Time Required: 2 to 4 months

Key activities include:

Drafting and signing the Development Agreement

Power of Attorney execution

Finalizing commercial terms (area, rent, amenities)

Delays often occur here if consensus among members is not achieved quickly.

3. Government Approvals & Permissions

Time Required: 4 to 5 months

This is the most time-consuming phase in Mumbai redevelopment.

Approvals required:

IOD (Intimation of Disapproval)

CC (Commencement Certificate)

Fire, environment, and aviation clearances (if applicable)

Municipal permissions

An experienced developer helps minimize delays during this stage.

4. Vacating the Building & Demolition

Time Required: 2 to 4 months

Once approvals are in place:

Residents vacate the building

Transit rent or temporary accommodation is provided

Demolition work begins

Proper planning ensures this phase is smooth and well-coordinated.

5. Construction Phase

Time Required: 18 to 30 months

Construction timelines depend on:

Number of floors

Construction technology used

Site conditions

Material availability

At Tatvm Group, strict project monitoring ensures quality construction within committed timelines.

6. Occupation Certificate & Possession

Time Required: 2 to 4 months

Final steps include:

Obtaining Occupation Certificate (OC)

Utility connections

Final handover to society members

After this, residents can move into their new homes.

Factors That Can Delay Redevelopment in Mumbai

Lack of 100% society member consent

Legal disputes or title issues

Delays in municipal approvals

Changes in government policies

Poor planning by the developer

Choosing a reliable and experienced developer significantly reduces these risks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can redevelopment be completed in less than 3 years?
In rare cases, yes—if approvals are fast and the project is small. However, 3–5 years is a realistic timeline.

Q. Who controls the redevelopment timeline?
The timeline depends on approvals, society cooperation, and the developer’s execution capability.

Q. Is transit rent paid during the entire redevelopment period?
Yes, transit rent is paid until possession as per the development agreement.
