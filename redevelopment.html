Redevelopment

Old buildings. New beginnings.

Redevelopment of aging housing societies across Mumbai — fair, transparent and on time.

Why redevelopment

Your society’s next fifty years.

Thousands of Mumbai societies sit on strong land and weakening structures. Redevelopment is how a building’s promise is renewed — without a family having to leave the neighbourhood it loves.

More space, same address

Members receive larger carpet areas and modern amenities — while keeping the community and the locality they are rooted in.

Structure & safety

New RCC construction, fire compliance and lifts replace ageing frames — engineered for the next fifty monsoons.

Transparent terms

Clear area entitlements, timelines and compensation in writing — so no family negotiates in the dark.

How it works

Society to skyline, in five steps.

  1. 1

    Talk & feasibility

    We study your society’s land, FSI potential and members’ wishes — no obligation.

  2. 2

    Consent & terms

    Member meetings, transparent area and rent/compensation terms, drafted with your legal advisors.

  3. 3

    Approvals

    Developer-side work: 79(A) registration, plans, RERA registration and municipal sanctions.

  4. 4

    Build & temporary homes

    Members move to arranged rented homes; we build with the five fundamentals — light, air, space, community, sustainability.

  5. 5

    Handover, together

    Corpus, new flats with OC, amenities and a society that stays whole — rooted, renewed.
