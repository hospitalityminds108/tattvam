Frequently asked

NRI questions, answered.

Can NRIs buy residential property in Mumbai?

Yes. Under FEMA, NRIs and OCIs can purchase residential property in India without RBI approval. We help you stay compliant at every step.

Can I buy without visiting India?

Mostly, yes — with a Power of Attorney, video walkthroughs and digital documentation. We support the POA drafting and attestation process.

How do payments work from abroad?

Funds move through NRE/NRO accounts or inward remittance via banking channels. We share a clear payment schedule before you commit.

Can Tat:vm manage the home afterwards?

Yes — handover, furnishing coordination and rental management support is available for NRI buyers.
