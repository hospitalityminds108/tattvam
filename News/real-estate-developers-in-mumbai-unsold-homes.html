Mumbai, October 2026: India’s stock of unsold homes has grown again, and Mumbai holds the largest share of it. A Hindustan Times report by Ateeq Shaikh, based on Liases Foras data, puts unsold residential inventory at over 865,000 units at the end of the first half of FY 2026-27, a rise of 2.7%. The report carries the views of Raahul Maroo, Founder & Managing Director of Tat:vm Builders & Developers Pvt Ltd., on what this situation means for developers and buyers.

Nearly one in three unsold homes in the country is in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. MMR also has the highest average price among the eight major cities tracked.

Supply is running ahead of sales.

MMR sales were flat in the quarter while launches rose by almost a fifth. The report notes a similar gap nationally, with the CRE Matrix estimating about 2.58 lakh homes sold against about 2.98 lakh launched in H1 2026.


Other cities were mixed. NCR saw the sharpest fall in sales at 2.4%, and its average price dropped 5.9% to ₹22,115 per sq ft because new launches were priced lower. Bengaluru slipped slightly. Pune, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, and Chennai gained momentum, and Kolkata was flat.

What Raahul Maroo told Hindustan Times

Mr Maroo told the paper that the coming few years will be a crucial test for the sector. Construction costs are going up, he said, but developers “cannot keep increasing prices indefinitely without testing affordability and demand.”


He referred to Warren Buffett’s distinction between price and value: the price a developer needs to charge and the price a buyer will accept are not always the same. In his view, the strain will show up in project margins and cash flows. Developers carrying heavy debt or working on thin margins may struggle to keep construction going, which can mean delays and, in some cases, stalled projects.

Developers are turning cautious.

The report describes a careful mood among MMR developers. One mid-sized developer from the western suburbs told the paper that the firm has slowed new acquisitions and is concentrating on completing what it already has in hand.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How many unsold homes are there in India right now?+

Over 865,000 units at the end of H1 FY 2026-27, according to Liases Foras data reported by the Hindustan Times. That is 2.7% higher than before.

2. How much of India’s unsold housing stock is in Mumbai?+

About 30%. The Mumbai Metropolitan Region has 260,629 unsold units, the largest share of any city region.

3. Why is unsold inventory rising in MMR?+

New supply is growing faster than sales. In Q2 of FY 2026-27, launches rose 18.7% to 30,262 units while sales stayed flat at 34,552 units.

4. Are property prices in Mumbai falling?+

No. The average weighted price in MMR rose 1.3% to ₹25,199 per sq ft, the highest among the eight major cities in the report.
