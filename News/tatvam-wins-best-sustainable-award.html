Tat:vm Builders & Developers is thrilled to announce that we have been recognized by the Indian Green Building Council (IGBC) with the prestigious Best Sustainable Development Award 2024. This honor reflects our unwavering commitment to building homes and commercial spaces that prioritize environmental responsibility, community well-being, and sustainable urban development.

Award Recognition

The award was presented during the IGBC Annual Summit on March 15, 2024, recognizing Tat:vm's innovative approach to real estate development. The judging panel commended our implementation of the Five Tattvas philosophy — integrating Space, Light, Air, Vastu harmony, and Sustainability into every project.

"This award is not just recognition for what we've accomplished — it's validation of our belief that sustainable development and economic viability are not mutually exclusive. They go hand-in-hand." — Founder & MD, Tat:vm Group

Sustainability Commitment

Our commitment to sustainability extends across multiple dimensions:

  • Water Conservation: All projects include rainwater harvesting and wastewater recycling systems, reducing municipal dependence by 30-40%
  • Energy Efficiency: Solar-ready infrastructure, LED lighting, and passive design reduce energy consumption by 25-35%
  • Material Selection: Locally-sourced, low-emission materials minimize transportation impact and support local economies
  • Green Spaces: Native plant landscaping and urban gardens enhance biodiversity while improving resident well-being
  • Community Integration: Affordable housing options and community facilities ensure inclusive, vibrant neighborhoods

Our Approach: The Five Tattvas

Unlike treating sustainability as an afterthought, we embed it into our foundational design philosophy through the Five Tattvas:

🌿 Space: Flexible, adaptable layouts that reduce the need for larger homes and unnecessary consumption of resources.

☀️ Light: Natural light prioritized in design before calculating saleable area, reducing daytime electricity consumption.

💨 Air: Cross-ventilation engineered into every unit, enabling passive cooling and eliminating constant air-conditioning dependency.

🕉️ Vastu: Ancient principles of harmony applied to modern architecture, creating homes that feel naturally balanced and healthy.

🌍 Sustainability: Water harvesting, solar infrastructure, waste management, and environmental stewardship built into every project from day one.

This integrated philosophy ensures that every aspect of our developments works in harmony with both residents and the environment.

Future Vision

The Best Sustainable Development Award 2024 motivates us to raise the bar even higher. We are committed to:

  • Expanding solar and renewable energy integration across all projects by 2025
  • Achieving net-zero water consumption in new developments through advanced recycling systems
  • Incorporating biodiversity corridors that connect urban green spaces
  • Supporting circular economy principles through waste reduction and material reuse
  • Engaging communities in sustainable practices through education and partnerships

We believe that the real estate industry plays a critical role in shaping the future of cities like Mumbai. By choosing sustainability today, we're building a more livable, prosperous, and environmentally responsible tomorrow.

Thank you to the Indian Green Building Council for this recognition, and to our team, partners, and residents who share our vision of sustainable urban development. This award belongs to all of us.
